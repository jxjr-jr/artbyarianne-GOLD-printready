Works by Jas Rogers

Gallery

01

Studio Session

Ink on paper — 2024

Viewed from above, a figure is absorbed in quiet work at a cluttered desk. Plants crowd the windowsill behind them, cards are spread across the floor, and a small heart floats in the corner — a moment of focused, loving labour.

02

Three of a Kind

Ink on paper — 2024

Three monkeys cling together on a single branch, their fur rendered in dense, expressive crosshatching. Two small hearts frame the composition — an affectionate, lively study of togetherness and shared weight.

03

Taking Stock

Ink on paper — 2024

A figure kneels on a bare floor, surrounded by the accumulated objects of a life — bags, books, shoes, small things. There is patience in the posture and a quiet heart drawn nearby to mark the moment.

04

At the Easel

Ink on paper — 2024

An artist sits before a large canvas depicting animals on a landscape, flanked by potted plants on either side. Seen only from behind, the figure is fully absorbed — a portrait of the creative act from the outside in.

05

Together

Ink on paper — 2024

A bearded man and a woman with long hair and glasses sit close, a trailing plant cascading above them. Three small hearts are scattered through the composition — an intimate, unhurried sketch of companionship.

06

Close Work

Ink on paper — 2024

A curly-haired figure leans in close to their canvas, face inches from the surface, scrutinising. The profile is sharp and intent. A small heart sits quietly in the upper corner — devotion made visible.

07

Spin

Ink on paper — 2024

A lone figure on a stationary bike, cap on, leaning into the handlebars. The machinery is rendered with clean, economic lines while the rider is dark and weighted — effort and stillness occupying the same frame.

08

Hands & Chair

Ink on paper — 2024

Two hands meet at the centre of the frame, holding something tiny between them — a small insect, a found thing. Behind them, the dense vertical hatching of a upholstered chair fills the frame with texture and weight.
