The artist

Jas

Jas is an independent artist working primarily in acrylic. Her practice centres on the accurate representation of fabrics and textiles, exploring the subtle interplay of light, pattern, and form.

She finds most of her subjects in warm interior spaces — in vintage furniture, utility fabrics, and the lived-in textures of domestic comfort. Each work is a careful study of material and moment.

Medium Acrylic on paper
Based in Melbourne, Australia
