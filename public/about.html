My Creative Practice

I’m not yet a full‑fledged artist who creates everything freehand from scratch — though I hope to be one day. For now, I do my best to capture and preserve patterns and colour sequences created by others, especially in materials I’m deeply drawn to. Re‑creating these designs in vibrant, like‑for‑like paintings brings me delight. It shines a light on what already brings me joy, doubling it and giving the original design a well‑deserved shout‑out. (I use basic acrylic paints on black paper, and offer reproduction prints that match the size of the original.)

Simple dot mandalas are also calming for me. (I’ve enjoyed using acrylic paint on canvas, black or brown paper, and painted rocks. These aren’t for sale — I usually keep dot painting as an occasional way to hand‑make cards.)

Another dimension of my creative work is self‑expression and healing through basic sketching. When intuitive thoughts and emotions overwhelm me, drawing them out with a fine‑liner pen on high‑quality art paper helps them exit my mind and exist somewhere else. Not just separate from me, but in a dignified, understandable way for anyone experiencing something similar — and even as a way to explain to someone who may never truly know what it feels like. (These sketches aren’t for sale; they’re private journaling pieces shared only with those close to me.)

Healing art isn’t only for difficult moments. I also use it to focus on positives — joy, gratitude, and the captured moments of my highs. These pieces express my innermost motives and love, motivated by loyalty and commitment. They are a thank‑you to John for becoming the person I love and trust, and a hope that we can continue building happiness together, eternally united in joy. They’re about capturing new, pure memories: noticing them, recalling them, cherishing them. (These small fine‑liner sketches, often based on photos on my phone, are given to John as little “work snack notes.” Not for sale, but happy to share.)

About me:

Born in 1998 with Dutch, English, Italian, and Maltese heritage, I grew up in Melbourne’s inner‑west and now live in Moonee Ponds, VIC. I’m happily settled here with my handsome husband, who shares my spiritual identity as one of Jehovah’s Witnesses. I’m also a qualified disability support worker, grateful for the chance to assist wonderful individuals I genuinely connect with.

Art has been a joy since childhood, and that passion never faded. Although I’m not a career artist, I rediscovered the hobby as an adult and reignited the satisfaction that comes from creating. I rarely go long without a painting project underway — even if the larger pieces sometimes wait weeks for me to return to them. They sit patiently, whilst I grow impatient wanting a chance to work on them again. At least I can draw in the meantime if I so feel inclined or have the need to therapeutically.

Based in Melbourne, Australia
